> **Note on the SHA delta.** `86f41294` adds the cleaning-plan feature
> (commits `b200812..86f4129`). Inner-loop bench numbers are 5–15 %
> higher than the older SHA. The cleaning-plan plumbing adds new
> helpers in `edatime-query/src/cleaning.rs` and
> `edatime-query/src/filters.rs` that the bench harness pays for even
> when the work is the same. We accept the regression as the new
> floor: subsequent phases optimise against the cleaning-plan SHA,
> not the older one.

## Things to NOT do with this artefact

- **Do not** treat the older `20260714T082822Z` numbers as the
  baseline — they were captured on a pre-cleaning-plans SHA and
  pre-fix procedure. Use only `20260714T150000Z` for Phase 1 onwards.
- **Do not** cherry-pick a single bench number; report the full
  three-axis view (inner loop / HTTP / telemetry) so future
  reviewers can spot which axis moved.
- **Do not** drop the `cpu_admission_pending` field from the
  result JSON. It is the leading indicator for spawn_blocking
  saturation and the only check that the run actually drained
  before snapshotting.
