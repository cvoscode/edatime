> Icon-only button for toolbars.

## Function: IconButton
- `IconButton(props: IconButtonProps): HTMLButtonElement`

## IconButtonProps
- `icon: string`
- `ariaLabel: string`
- `onClick?: () => void`
- `className?: string`
- `title?: string`
